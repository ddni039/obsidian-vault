# Josef and Anni Albers Foundation

Non-profit organization founded 1971 by [[josef-albers]] and his wife [[anni-albers]] to preserve and promote their artistic and design legacy.

## Overview

- **Founded**: 1971, by Josef Albers and Anni Albers
- **Location**: Orange, Connecticut (Josef and Anni's home)
- **Purpose**: Preserve, exhibit, and educate about the Albers' work
- **Note**: First living artist (Josef) to receive solo show at the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1971)

## Activities

- Preserves the *Homage to the Square* archive
- Maintains the Albers' home and studio
- Provides access to researchers and scholars
- Licenses images of Albers' work for publication
- Supports education in color theory and textile arts
